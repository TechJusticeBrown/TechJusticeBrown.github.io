Short description that explains how the event aligns with JTAG's principles:
    - A political-economic investigation of AI  
        - AI in the history of thought in work, automation, and ownership, as well as the history of capitalism  
            - Capitalism and Computers in the Era of A.I. https://cceai.ohrg.org/syllabus.pdf  
        - AI in contemporary capitalism:  
            - the materiality and the capitalist making of AI: supply chains (compute divide, data workers, etc.)  
            - AI's impact: replacement, informalization, structural shifts in industrial organization and corresponding labor organization  
    - How should we respond to AI  
        - secure, decentralized, and participatory practices in the development and usage of LLMs  
            - The Open Anonymity Project for anonymous LLM inferences https://openanonymity.ai/  
            - Federated learning?  
            - participatory AI practices  
        - reimagining AI (not necessarily LLMs!) 4 liberation & socialism:  
            - sovereign AI https://interactives.cnas.org/reports/sovereign-ai-index/  
            - public AI? https://www.aipotluck.org/  
            - Cybersyn https://thereader.mitpress.mit.edu/project-cybersyn-chiles-radical-experiment-in-cybernetic-socialism/
