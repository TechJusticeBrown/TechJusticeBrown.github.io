**Projects**
+ Surveillence Pricing: Recently, [https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/maryland-becomes-the-first-state-to-restrict-surveillance-pricing](Maryland) and [https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/politics/seattle-mayor-signs-grocery-fair-pricing-into-law-bans-surveillance/](Seattle) banned sureveillence pricing. We want to ask: how effective are these laws in practice? And how can we bring that to Providence/RI? 
+ Flock Cameras: Flock (and ALPRs at large) are bad for privacy. As of right now, five RI towns have [https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/40356986-72f9-48cf-939e-895b4ec95b4b](terminated contracts with Flock), Providence is considering [https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/cdeb58b7-dc50-42c3-84c2-e151ef3cb9a8](joining them), and the Rhode Island Govenor's report is set to come out [https://governor.ri.gov/press-releases/governor-mckee-takes-action-automated-license-plate-readers-calls-pause-new](any day now). What might good state-level legislation look like?
